| **Motion-Separated ADS-B Integrity and Turn-Diversity Audit** | Stage 4H-A5 separates evidence that a report is internally consistent from evidence that an aircraft is maneuvering. The reproducible legacy policy remains the default, while `integrity_motion_separation_v2` compares each measured displacement with the trapezoidal mean of its endpoint velocities. Training aircraft alone fit strict/default/permissive 3/5/8 scaled-MAD endpoint thresholds. Event causes use all constituent pairs regardless of survival or fragmentation. Verification recomputes v1 against immutable A4, hashes the native transitive dependency closure, enforces canonical output plus a verified archive, scans every production dependency for training or Q/R selection, and combines A5 checks with all nine A3 decision gates. Test data is used only by the predeclared diversity gate. | `runStage4HTurnDiversityAudit.m`, `helperBuildStage4HQualityManifest.m`, `helperAuditStage4HTurnDiversity.m`, `helperBuildStage4HDependencyManifest.m`, `helperVerifyStage4HLegacyManifest.m`, `helperBuildStage4HFinalGateTable.m`, `helperPlotStage4HTurnDiversityAudit.m`, `stage4HTurnDiversityAuditLiveScript.m`, `tests/Stage4HTurnDiversityAuditTest.m` |
| **Cartesian ADS-B Velocity Synchronization and Controlled Rebuild** | Stage 4H-A4 treats reported course as a circular direction rather than a scalar: each MSG,4 speed/course sample becomes east and north velocity, those components are interpolated independently with no extrapolation, and speed/course are reconstructed afterward. A field-compatibility gate protects timestamps, positions, altitude, vertical rate, grouping, finite masks, state-pair usability, and bistatic projection. The isolated rebuild preserves all non-horizontal dataset content and matches the independent A3 Cartesian oracle to `1.91e-13 m/s`. Corrected A3 clears ingestion integrity but still fails clean-core maneuver diversity, yielding `collect_or_revise_audit` with no neural training. | `../BistaticDataAnalysis/loadADSBTruth.m`, `buildLocalADSBStatePairDataset.m`, `runStage4HADSBIngestionRepair.m`, `runStage4HADSBIntegrityGate.m`, `stage4HADSBIntegrityGateLiveScript.m`, `tests/LoadADSBTruthFieldCompatibilityTest.m`, `tests/LoadADSBTruthSyntheticTest.m` |
| **ADS-B Supervision Integrity Gate** | Stage 4H-A3 traces every one of the 457,903 frozen state-pair rows back to the raw position and velocity reports used to construct both endpoints, converts speed/course/vertical rate to Cartesian ENU before interpolation, and assigns strict, default, and permissive quality tiers without deleting or replacing values. | **Training-Only EKF Noise Calibration and Frozen Gate Rerun** | Stage 4H-A2 standardizes the causal replay on `trackingEKF` with native `constvel`/`constveljac` and `cvmeas`/`cvmeasjac`, while retaining a small Cartesian-velocity measurement adapter for asynchronous ADS-B speed/course/vertical-rate reports. A bounded Q/R sensitivity search scores only 120 training aircraft and requires pair-weighted position RMSE, position P95, and velocity noninferiority to hold simultaneously. | **Causal Posterior-Conditioned Prediction Gate** | Stage 4H-A reconstructs a conventional linear `trackingKF` posterior from asynchronous SBS MSG,3 and MSG,4 events before deciding whether to train another neural transition. Target velocities are also interpolated in Cartesian ENU to avoid heading-seam corruption.
