**Finding:** at 6000 ft AGL, WLEK provides ideal useful 3D geometry over about 1103.3 km² of the northern ROI, but RF-supported gain on the 0.02° grid is only 0.3297 km² from a partial boundary cell whose center lies outside the ROI. Both finer grids give zero northern gain. West-corridor 3D gains are small and grid-sensitive (0.0916, 0.5038, 1.0992 km² at 0.02°, 0.01°, 0.005°). No robust material northern benefit is demonstrated. Catalog Newton does not change that recommendation.
