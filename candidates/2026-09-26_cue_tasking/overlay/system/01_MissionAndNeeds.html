System engineering · step 1

Mission and Stakeholder Needs

What the testbed is for, who needs what from it, and the needs that requirements must serve

Status snapshot: 26 September 2026. The masters of everything summarised here are in flightTest docs/system/; where a page and a master disagree, the master wins.

Purpose

Bottom line (ELI5): Before designing anything, write down what the testbed is for and who needs what from it. Everything else in this section traces back to these two goals.

Claim boundary: goals and needs state intent. They are not evidence that the system meets them; that is recorded against each need's status and on the verification page.

Mission goals

IDGoal
MG-1Collect passive radar data to show that a complete passive radar system can be built with MATLAB and MathWorks tools.
MG-2Collect data that proves MATLAB functions, for example radar, tracking and signal processing, work as designed on real data that can be shared with customers.

MG-1 is about the system: a complete passive radar chain built and run with MATLAB and MathWorks tools. MG-2 is about the data: real recordings, with truth, good enough to show that MATLAB functions behave as designed, and packaged so they can be shared with customers. The exploration reports (Report 01 onward) explore whether the physics and processing make that possible; this section defines the system that has to deliver it.
