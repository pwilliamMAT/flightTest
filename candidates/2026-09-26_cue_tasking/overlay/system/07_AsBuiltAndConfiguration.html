Time source

Accepted: internet NTP
  • chrony on the Pi keeps time from internet NTP, routed through the collection desktop.
  • Stated tolerance: 0.1 s between the Pi and the collection desktop (accepted by the owner on 26 September 2026, DR-TIME-1). Basis: the one check after NTP was reachable found +0.05 to +0.09 s, limited by SSH jitter; chrony reported 0.3 ms to its source.
  • Why 0.1 s is enough for now: at up to about 290 m/s, 0.1 s changes the bistatic range by at most about 58 m, about one range cell for a ~5.4 MHz ATSC signal.
To do and open
  • Hardware to-do: GPS/PPS reference clocks are configured but not locked; a loose component is suspected. Reseat and re-check (A-2, Leif; DR-TIME-3). Not a blocker for NTP-based timing.
  • Open: the Pi was measured 14.9 s slow before 2026-09-25 21:07 UTC. ADS-B truth recorded before then, including the 2026-09-25 tracking scan, is not timing truth until its offset is bounded (A-3, Pat; DR-TIME-4).
  • Open: no package yet records the hosts' time-sync state (DR-TIME-2).
