System engineering · step 2

System Requirements (Draft Baseline)

From mission goals to derived requirements, with allocation, verification method and status

Status snapshot: 26 September 2026. The masters of everything summarised here are in flightTest docs/system/; where a page and a master disagree, the master wins.

DRAFT requirements baseline for owner review (proposed by CR-10, 26 September 2026). No requirement is accepted yet. Values marked TBD are unknown; the one proposed value (DR-TIME-1, 0.1 s) cites its measurement.

Purpose

Bottom line (ELI5): Until now the testbed had a design but no written list of what it must do. This baseline starts from the two mission goals and works down, writing requirements only where the goals or existing design decisions already call for them, and marks every unknown value as TBD rather than guessing.

Levels: mission goals (MG) → mission needs (MN) → system requirements (SR) → derived requirements (DR). Each requirement has a rationale, a parent, an allocation to architecture items, a verification method (I inspection, A analysis, D demonstration, T test), and a status on current evidence: Verified evidence shows it met; Partial part met, or met under narrower conditions; Not met the item exists and does not meet it; Not started the allocated item is not built; Proposed depends on a rule or message that is itself only proposed.

Claim boundary: a requirement's status reflects the evidence cited in its row on 26 September 2026. Verified requirements so far concern the cue message interface; no requirement about detection, tracking, or dataset release is met.
