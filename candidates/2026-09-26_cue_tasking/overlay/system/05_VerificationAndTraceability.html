Verification and Traceability

How each requirement is checked, what has been checked, and where the evidence is

Status snapshot: 26 September 2026. The masters of everything summarised here are in flightTest docs/system/; where a page and a master disagree, the master wins.

Purpose

Bottom line (ELI5): For each requirement: how will we check it, has it been checked, and where is the proof? So far the checks that passed are all about the cue messages; the radar parts are still to be verified.

Claim boundary: verification here is requirement-level. A Verified cue-interface requirement shows messages arrive intact and valid; it does not show that a cued collection yields a detection. Evidence classes follow the reporting rules (modeled, installed, measured, synthetic, diagnostic, operational); no operational evidence exists.
