1. Purpose and current status

Bottom line (ELI5): The project has built and tested important parts of the “listen, compare, and make an aircraft measurement” chain. It is not allowed to call the chain ready yet. A large, integrity-valid map test failed, so the next work is to review and verify one bounded correction before restarting that test. This is the gate system doing its job: it protects later detection, tracking, and localization work from being built on an untrusted map.

The north star is a trustworthy passive-bistatic capability that can detect and track aircraft in field data, characterize where one link can provide useful measurements, and establish honest evidence for later multi-measurement localization. The active milestone is G4-R map-rate validity and field-readiness closure. As of 2026-09-18, the formal 108-condition R5A matrix failed its frozen map-rate criteria. A native-first, calibration-trained then frozen 16-tap NLMS path is supported as a bounded upstream R4 correction candidate, but it has not been adopted into production R4 or verified under the current production contract.

5. Why the status matters

Bottom line (ELI5): Tracking and localization are only as trustworthy as the aircraft measurements supplied to them. A good-looking map, a synthetic demonstration, or a collection photograph cannot substitute for a validated detector measurement with known timing, geometry, and uncertainty.

This program intentionally separates four questions: whether the analysis path is implemented, whether a bounded result is demonstrated, whether a diagnostic result supports a narrow finding, and whether a formal gate has opened. The distinction prevents infrastructure or diagnostic evidence from being promoted into aircraft-performance evidence. It also keeps a useful result—such as the cause of a map mismatch—from being discarded merely because it does not yet authorize a detector.

Those counts make the diagnostic work reviewable; they do not establish live-aircraft detection, operational Pd, or operational Pfa.

7. Figures and visual evidence: what the strongest numbers say

Bottom line (ELI5): The important contrast is not “one plot looked good and another looked bad.” The R4 fixed pilot met predeclared map-contract limits, then the broader R5A matrix did not. The next correction candidate is promising only because a separate, bounded characterization isolated a specific order-of-operations difference.

R4 and R5A answer different questions. The R4 fixed pilot checks a frozen configuration at one prescribed condition; the R5A matrix checks the same Passive Map contract across 108 paired conditions. The matrix failed peak agreement, correlation, energy-difference, Direct Path/ridge, and classification-stability conditions. The cancellation characterization then tested a fixed family of methods and found that native-rate 16-tap NLMS trained on target-free calibration and frozen before scoring preserved the tested map contract; the current independently reset native/reduced comparator did not.

Figures and visual evidence

Engineering interpretation: treating the R5A failure as a real, preserved result is important. The evidence-supported next move is not to widen a detector, select an LMS product, or collect a headline performance figure. It is to review the specific native-first correction, verify it under the existing R4 contract and regressions, and only then consider a separately authorized replacement R5A matrix.

9. Supported diagnostic findings

Bottom line (ELI5): Diagnostic evidence is valuable because it narrows the next question. It does not answer a bigger question than the experiment was designed to answer.

The cancellation characterization is the most direct current diagnostic. The native-first frozen-NLMS candidate preserved the tested common-grid map contract across nine real controls and a 15 km map-preservation guard. In contrast, the independently reset native/reduced NLMS comparator did not preserve the map contract in every real control. This supports review of rate/order/reset semantics; it does not establish a mitigation-product winner or hardware adequacy.

The G6-D and synthetic studies are also bounded. G6-D had structural and retention checks, but its recommendation is provisional. The 15 km synthetic support study showed that historical support excluded that known synthetic truth; the diagnostic replay changed only a support boundary and is not a production detector change. PBR-SVE-001 Phase A quantified sampled ADS-B geometry opportunity, not detection or localization coverage.

11. Meaning and interpretation

Bottom line (ELI5): The program has converted a vague “can we see aircraft?” question into a narrower, testable sequence: make the map physically comparable, decide whether the collection is suitable, freeze an input product, validate a truth-blind detector, then evaluate trackers and localization with real measurement contracts.

The failed matrix and the bounded correction candidate point to a controlled engineering decision, not an open-ended search. The original Passive Map comparison defect was explained; the R5A failure then identified behavior that the native-first ordering may correct. The next verification must preserve the existing map metrics, parity, source fingerprints, and regressions. It must not silently change criteria or treat synthetic-target association as a substitute for matrix validation. STAT-003 STAT-004

The collection branch is independently important. ADS-B opportunity analysis can say that a specified rectangle contains 42.559% of sampled opportunity, but it cannot determine whether IQ/ADS-B timing is sufficiently aligned or whether the Reference Path was suitable for a formal field result. That requires a reviewed policy and traceable acquisition evidence. Physical installation and package evidence should be read in Report 02, not inferred from analysis artifacts. STAT-006

12. Next required milestones and future paths

Bottom line (ELI5): The next milestone is deliberately small: decide whether to adopt one reviewed correction and prove that it preserves the existing map contract. Only after that proof can the program decide whether to spend resources on a replacement matrix. Tracking and localization are future paths, not the next task.

The future tracking path is: validated map formation and collection suitability → formal G5 product decision → formal G6 frozen detector input → formal G7 time/geometry truth windows → formal G8 truth-blind detector validation and measurement contract → tracker readiness → tracking and truth evaluation. The localization path begins only after tracking-quality measurements and suitable multi-observation geometry are available. One-link opportunity analysis is useful context but is not localization-capable coverage.

Broadcast aircraft-surveillance data used here as post-hoc timing and geometry context, not to steer the detector. Since 2026-09 it also feeds the Cue Tasker, which may select collection opportunities but must not steer map formation, thresholding, or detection.
Cue Tasker (CT)
The service on the ADS-B Raspberry Pi that publishes one cue per aircraft: predicted path and up to eight ranked (receive site, DTV illuminator) opportunities with modeled, pre-integration SNR windows.
CFAR
Constant false-alarm rate processing. Diagnostic CFAR results are not an operational false-alarm measurement.
Direct Path
The strong broadcast component received through the direct transmitter-to-receiver route. It is not the same thing as the Reference Path.
G1–G10
The ordered project gates from ingest through tracking/truth evaluation. They are defined in the project README and detailed in Report 03.
G4-R / R1–R5B
The bounded G4 remediation and revalidation sequence. R5A tests map formation; R5B separately assesses collection suitability.
Map-rate validity
Whether native-rate and reduced-rate map products are physically comparable under an explicit common-grid contract.
Measurement
A validated output governed by a declared Measurement contract with relevant timing, geometry, and uncertainty information. No such Measurement is established here.
Native-first frozen NLMS
A candidate cancellation order: train a 16-tap normalized LMS filter on target-free calibration at native rate, freeze adaptation for scoring, then resample the residual. It is not a selected G5 product.
objectDetection
A MATLAB detection-object output. It remains unavailable because formal G8 is blocked.
Passive Map
The passive cross-ambiguity map produced from the Reference Path and Surveillance Path. It is an upstream analysis product, not by itself a validated Measurement.
Pd / Pfa
Probability of detection / probability of false alarm. No operational Pd or Pfa is established in this report.
Reference Path
The declared receiver path intended to provide a usable copy of the HDTV broadcast for comparison. It is not synonymous with the Direct Path, and its collection suitability remains blocked.
R4 checkpoint
The frozen verification and fixed-pilot stage within G4-R. The earlier checkpoint passed; the current candidate correction still requires adoption and verification.
R5A matrix
The exact 108-condition bounded map-formation validation matrix. Its failure is retained as authoritative current evidence.
R5B
The separately reviewed collection-suitability assessment based on ADS-B timing and geometry policy; it remains blocked.
Surveillance Path
The declared receiver path intended to observe the scene together with broadcast content, including possible reflected components. It is paired with the Reference Path to form a Passive Map.
Supported Diagnostic
A status indicating that a bounded experiment supports a narrow engineering conclusion but does not promote a formal gate or operational claim.
Tracking / Localization
Future uses of validated Measurements. Tracking needs an established Measurement contract; Localization additionally needs suitable multi-observation geometry. Neither is validated in this report.
