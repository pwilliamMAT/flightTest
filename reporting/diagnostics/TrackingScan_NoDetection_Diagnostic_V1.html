Aircraft-tracking scan: no truth-matched track

A four-part tracking scan at 599 MHz (the Newton ATSC transmitter) ran cleanly and was analysed end to end, but the detector matched the one overlapping ADS-B aircraft once in 75 opportunities. 91% of the 34,054 detections are 60 Hz hum lines, and most of the rest are further low-Doppler lines. At the aircraft's Doppler the detector is quiet, yet the aircraft is still absent, even when its predicted track is stacked over all 75 blocks. The geometry was favourable, so the missing sensitivity points at the processing chain, which is the next thing to diagnose. The scan also exposed a hard-coded transmitter position and a memory limit that had crashed an earlier analysis.
