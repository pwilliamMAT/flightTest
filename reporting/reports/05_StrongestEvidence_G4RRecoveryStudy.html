1. Purpose and current evidence status

Bottom line (ELI5): This report asks a deliberately small question: when a known, simulated aircraft echo is placed into a real HDTV recording, can the fixed diagnostic detector inspect the expected map cell and retain a matching candidate? The strongest result is that the old 15 km case was excluded by the search boundary, not observed to fail. Under a diagnostic grid-derived boundary, the native-first frozen-NLMS path associated all eight available injected geometries after truth-blind detection; the no-mitigation comparison did not. This is synthetic recovery evidence, not live-aircraft evidence.

Report 05 owns the strongest current experimental evidence package: the G4-R close-target atlas and recovery grid. It is a compact, integrity-checked study built from real field background data, controlled synthetic injections, deterministic condition selection, fixed detector settings, paired untouched controls, and post-hoc-only truth association. It does not own the causal G4-R map-rate recovery journey (Report 04), hardware deployment (Report 02), or the project plan (Report 06).

EV-001 The authoritative current-source bundle declares 11 condition slots: two regenerated anchors and a 3 × 3 support/CFAR grid. Eight conditions were available and evaluated; three deliberately remain unavailable because no accepted Phase-A state met the required full-CFAR-geometry contract. No nearby substitute was used.

Strongest demonstrated fact

At fixed diagnostic settings, native-first frozen NLMS produced an accepted, post-hoc-associated candidate for all 8/8 available injected geometries.

Critical diagnostic distinction

The historical 15 km cell was outside historical CUT support. It was excluded; it was not a detector miss.

Current formal boundary

Production-support changes, formal G5/G6/G8, live-aircraft detection, tracking, and localization remain Blocked.

2. Executive summary

Bottom line (ELI5): The experiment put known synthetic targets into the surveillance channel of a real field recording, formed maps and detector decisions without looking at target truth, then checked afterward whether a retained candidate landed where the target had been placed. The evidence shows a support-policy exclusion at 15 km and a controlled recovery pattern under one fixed configuration. It does not show that the system detects live aircraft or has an operational probability of detection or false alarm.

Question

Was the close 15 km fixture barred by the historical search region, or did the detector inspect and miss it? EV-001

Demonstrated

Grid-derived support made the 15 km case eligible; native-first frozen NLMS associated all 8 available injected truths. EV-007

Control result

No-mitigation had 0/8 accepted truth-window outcomes; the paired controls had 0/16 associated nuisance outcomes. EV-007

What is bounded

One recorded background, fixed injection level, fixed CFAR/NMS, diagnostic support policy, and a small deterministic grid. EV-002

Management meaning

The study is strong diagnostic evidence for support-policy and controlled recovery reasoning; it cannot select a production product or validate operations. EV-011

4. Experimental question

Bottom line (ELI5): Imagine a search flashlight that only shines on part of a wall. If a target is just outside the lit area, saying “we did not see it” would be misleading—the target was never searched. This study asks whether the close target was outside the search area, and what happens when a fixed diagnostic extension makes that cell searchable.

EV-001 The close-target recovery question has four parts:

  • Close-target recovery: test the 15 km receiver-local anchor whose expected map location is −76.456 µs, 540.5 Hz. Its selected grid CUT is −65.104 µs, 540 Hz.
  • Injected-target strategy: place a controlled synthetic echo into a real one-second HDTV field background, then compare that altered recording with an otherwise untouched control recording.
  • Truth-blind evaluation: form the map, apply CA-CFAR, and perform NMS without generated trajectory truth. Reveal target prediction only afterward to ask whether a retained candidate falls in a declared delay–Doppler association window.
  • Support eligibility: determine first whether the target’s selected cell is inside the defined CUT search rectangle and has a full fixed CFAR footprint. A cell outside this contract receives no detector decision.

The historical CUT support was [−1200, −150] µs × ±750 Hz. The diagnostic grid-derived 15 km override changed only the upper delay boundary to −48.828128 µs; it was not adopted into production.

EV-008Required interpretation: historical 15 km status is support exclusion, not detector miss. A detector miss can be discussed only after the expected target cell was eligible, evaluated, and failed to produce an accepted truth-window candidate.

Primary evidence: S05-01 method and 15 km result and S05-03 map placement table.

5. Experiment design

Bottom line (ELI5): The experiment changes a few things on purpose—target geometry, support category, and mitigation product—while holding the real background, injection recipe, detector configuration, and truth-separation rule fixed.

Background and target insertion EV-002One-second field session 20260622T102123, part 1. CH1 surveillance receives the synthetic echo; recorded CH2 reference is preserved. A separate seed copy is conditioned only for synthetic-echo construction. Echo amplitude is fixed at −18 dB.
Geometries EV-003Two regenerated anchors (15 km and 45 km) plus a deterministic 3 × 3 grid spanning newly searchable, boundary-transition, and interior-delay zones crossed with direct-path-influenced, zero-Doppler-influenced, and off-ridge local conditions.
Products compared EV-007none and native_first_frozen_nlms. The latter is a bounded R4 correction candidate; this experiment does not select it as a production product.
Detector held fixed EV-006CA-CFAR at configured Pfa=1e-3, 320 training cells, 341 total footprint cells, and fixed 7 × 3 Doppler-by-delay NMS. Fixed settings make comparisons interpretable; they do not make the settings operational.
Matched controls EV-007Each injected evaluation has an untouched paired control. The control check asks only whether an associated candidate occurs in that same target-specific window after the truth-blind detector sequence.
What stayed outside the experimentTruth never steered map formation, thresholding, CFAR, or NMS. The study did not tune production support, measure map-wide false alarms, create a track, or estimate an aircraft location.

The injection geometry is physically generated from transmitter–target–receiver excess path and Doppler, using a fractional-delay propagation model. The map uses a negative processing-delay convention for positive physical bistatic excess path. The original recorded channels remain the control background; only the defined synthetic echo is added to surveillance.

Primary method source: S05-01 authoritative walkthrough; all declared condition fields: S05-03 condition manifest.

11. Supported conclusions

Bottom line (ELI5): The evidence supports precise statements about this experiment. It is strongest when we say exactly what was tested, which condition was eligible, and which product/configuration produced the recorded outcome.

EV-008Support exclusion is not a detector miss. The historical 15 km target cell was outside the historical CUT rectangle. It was not evaluated in that policy, so it cannot be counted as a failed detection.
EV-007The fixed diagnostic recovery contrast is real and traceable. With the grid-derived 15 km support and fixed CFAR/NMS configuration, native-first frozen NLMS associated all eight available injected targets; no mitigation associated none.
EV-003Availability is meaningful evidence. Three grid strata were retained as unavailable because they lacked an accepted state with full CFAR geometry. They do not weaken the result by being hidden, and they do not enlarge it by being replaced.
EV-005Support policy changes what can be evaluated and what local conditions enter the search. The atlas quantifies changed opportunity and CFAR-geometry coverage, including increased direct-path and zero-Doppler-footprint overlap. It does not identify the right production rectangle.
EV-006The compact detector arithmetic is independently checked. The available CFAR audits use the same 320-training/341-footprint contract, with maximum observed independent-versus-toolbox relative local-noise difference of 1.7501e−7, below the documented 1e−6 tolerance.

Terminology used in this report: Associated means a truth-blind retained candidate fell in the declared truth window only after detection. Excluded means the selected cell was outside support and did not receive a detector decision. Unavailable means no declared condition could be instantiated with the required geometry. Missed means eligible and evaluated but no accepted candidate associated; it applies narrowly to the no-mitigation injected rows, not to the historical 15 km exclusion. Accepted requires threshold clearance and NMS retention before truth is consulted. Rejected means that target-window candidate did not satisfy this fixed acceptance chain. Bounded means every statement remains limited to the declared synthetic experiment.

12. What This Evidence Does NOT Establish

Bottom line (ELI5): A carefully placed practice target in a real recording is useful evidence, but it is not the same as seeing a real aircraft under operational conditions. This study deliberately cannot answer the operational questions below.

This study does not establish:
  • Live-aircraft detection. The target echo and trajectory were synthetically generated, even though the background was a real field recording.
  • Operational Pd. Eight available deterministic injected geometries are not an operational population, randomized trial, or environmental coverage sample.
  • Operational Pfa. Pfa=1e-3 is a configured CFAR setting. A zero associated nuisance result in 16 target-specific matched-control checks is not a map-wide or operational false-alarm measurement.
  • Tracking performance. The study creates no validated measurement stream, track, association history, or track-to-truth evaluation.
This study also does not establish:
  • Localization performance. It has neither validated aircraft measurements nor a multi-observation localization experiment.
  • Product selection. Native-first frozen NLMS is a bounded diagnostic/R4 correction candidate; the formal R5A map-rate matrix remains failed and downstream formal gates remain disabled.
  • A production detector-support policy. The grid-derived override is a diagnostic condition used to inspect the support consequence.
  • Hardware or site sufficiency. A compact replay/injection study does not qualify the reference chain, receiver state, collection quality, or surveillance coverage.

Binding boundary: S05-06 current project state records this atlas as a small fixed-configuration diagnostic; S05-07 decisions retain the formal downstream limits. Blocked

13. Why the evidence matters

Bottom line (ELI5): This package is valuable because it makes a disputed-looking result inspectable: it shows where the target was placed, whether the detector was allowed to look there, what the detector did before truth was revealed, and what the paired control did.

Controlled experimental design EV-002

A real background is held alongside an untouched control; injection, geometry, products, and detector configuration are explicit. This makes a narrow comparison reproducible rather than anecdotal.

Traceability EV-003

Condition IDs, colors, LLA trajectories, source-state selection, map placement, thumbnails, and scalar outcomes join across compact retained artifacts.

Preservation checks EV-010

Execution-plan snapshot hashing, local-link checks, compact retention, condition joins, and formal-disablement checks passed. The bundle retains no raw IQ or full maps.

Support-policy evaluation EV-005

The atlas reports the support trade-off before making a recovery claim. It distinguishes an unavailable cell from an evaluated failure.

Detector independence EV-006

An independent CFAR-stencil calculation agrees with phased.CFARDetector2D local-noise estimates for every available row within declared tolerance.

Control comparisons EV-007

The evidence keeps product contrast and matched-control nuisance checks separate from operational false-alarm claims.

This is why the atlas is one of the project’s strongest evidence packages: it gives an auditable causal chain from condition design to eligibility to truth-blind detector output to post-hoc interpretation. Its strength comes from disciplined limits as much as from the 8/8 diagnostic recovery outcome.

16. Glossary

Associated
A truth-blind retained candidate lies in the target-specific delay–Doppler association window only after map formation, CFAR, and NMS are complete.
CA-CFAR
Cell-averaging constant-false-alarm-rate thresholding. In this study its configured Pfa=1e-3 is not an operational Pfa result.
Control
The paired untouched field capture evaluated with the same product and target-specific post-hoc association window. It is a matched nuisance check, not a map-wide false-alarm survey.
Detector miss
An eligible target cell was evaluated but no accepted candidate associated with its declared truth window. It must not describe a support-excluded condition.
Excluded
Outside the declared CUT search support. No detector decision is made at that selected target cell.
Grid-derived 15 km override
Diagnostic delay support [−1200, −48.828128] µs × ±750 Hz used to evaluate the close-target support consequence. It is not production support.
Native-first frozen NLMS
The bounded cancellation ordering used as a diagnostic comparison: train a 16-tap normalized LMS filter on disjoint calibration, freeze it for scoring at native rate, then resample the residual. It is not a selected product.
NMS
Nonmaximum suppression. Here, a fixed 7 × 3 Doppler-by-delay neighborhood is used after thresholding; NMS retention alone is not target acceptance.
Post-hoc truth
Use of generated target prediction only after the detector’s truth-blind map/CFAR/NMS decisions. Truth must not steer detection.
Support eligibility
Whether a selected CUT lies in the stated delay–Doppler rectangle and has the required CFAR evaluation geometry. It is separate from detection success.
Synthetic recovery evidence
Evidence from a deliberately generated echo inserted into real background data. It can test a controlled chain but does not establish live-aircraft performance.
Unavailable
No accepted Phase-A state could populate a declared stratum while meeting full-CFAR-geometry requirements. It is not a negative result.
