Report 02A · controlled synthetic experiment

How known aircraft-like echoes are created inside real HDTV recordings

A human-readable account of declared truth, delay/Doppler placement, surveillance-only injection, traceable session packaging, and the limits of a synthetic recovery experiment.

Executive answer: Looking only for real aircraft leaves a basic question unanswered: where and when should the target have appeared in the map? This experiment starts with a real HDTV recording, declares a trajectory before processing, calculates the echo delay and Doppler, adds that echo only to the surveillance channel, and preserves the original reference and a paired control. It can prove that a declared synthetic case is traceable through a controlled experiment. It cannot prove live-aircraft detection, operational false-alarm performance, hardware suitability, tracking, or localization.

1. Executive answer

Why not just look for real aircraft?
A real scene does not provide a known target path, echo strength, time, or expected map cell. A missing candidate can therefore mean many things.
What uncertainty does truth remove?
It removes uncertainty about the intended target geometry. The same declared truth produces the injected echo, the session metadata, and the post-hoc evaluation target—without steering detector generation.
What can this prove?
That a known, declared synthetic case can be placed in real HDTV background and packaged for a controlled, truth-blind downstream experiment.
What can it never prove?
It cannot establish a measured aircraft echo, live-aircraft detection, operational Pd/Pfa, false-alarm rate, a qualified Reference Path, collection suitability, tracking, or localization.

2. Why synthetic truth exists

Real recordings are valuable

They contain the actual HDTV background, clutter, interference, and channel relationship that a clean simulation misses.

Real aircraft are ambiguous

A live aircraft usually does not bring a known echo gain, known scattering behavior, a declared map cell, or a stable paired control scene.

Synthetic truth controls one question

It asks: given this declared path and this unchanged background, does the downstream experiment put any retained candidate near the declared location?

A controlled target is not a fake field trial. It intentionally removes ambiguity about target placement, then leaves the real recording in the background. That is why it is useful for a narrow, inspectable experiment—and why it cannot answer every field-performance question.

3. Engineering decision chain

Each step produces an artifact that the next step needs. The chain is deliberately designed so the known answer travels with the package but does not steer map formation or detection.

4. Step-by-step echo generation

1Choose a real backgroundLoad the recorded dual-channel session. Its clutter and direct-path context remain part of the experiment.
2Declare the targetGenerate synthetic waypoints or normalize capture-backed ADS-B into one truth bundle before replay.
3Calculate the geometryUse transmitter-to-target and target-to-receiver paths. Excess range determines delay; range rate determines Doppler.
4Build the echoApply wideband propagation, fractional delay, Doppler phase rotation, RMS normalization, and declared gain.
5Keep channels coherentFor real background, inject only CH1 surveillance and preserve CH2 reference. The paired control changes neither.
6Write reviewable filesWrite the dual-channel data, truth JSON, manifest, configuration, roles, and validation/readback information.
7Process truth-blindThe map, CFAR, and NMS run without using the target answer. Truth is used only after candidates exist.
8Associate afterwardCompare retained candidates to declared windows and support eligibility without retroactively changing the detector.

1

Declare the target before building the signal

Question

Can the experiment define target motion before any downstream signal-processing outcome is known?

Result

The generator creates a truth bundle from synthetic waypoints or capture-backed ADS-B, including motion and output records for later package use.

Why it matters

A known intended target removes the ambiguity of deciding after the fact whether a map feature “looks like” an aircraft.

Engineering decision

Declare and save truth first; reuse it for echo synthesis and only later for post-hoc association.

Evidence

Truth-generation code, the seed-backed walkthrough, trajectory visual, and package truth JSON/manifest contract. SRC-02A-01/02/03/07

Method

Normalize the truth source into target position, velocity, timing, and metadata records that can feed geometry and file-writing helpers.

Remaining uncertainty

The trajectory describes the synthetic target placed into the experiment. It does not calibrate aircraft scattering, receive power, or a live-aircraft path.

Result

The code turns excess path into delay and excess-path rate into Doppler. For the current 15 km atlas anchor, the expected location is −76.456 µs and 540.5 Hz; the selected CUT is −65.104 µs and 540 Hz.Why it matters: placement becomes an inspectable geometric question rather than a search for arbitrary bright pixels.Conclusion: the study can declare an expected delay-Doppler neighborhood before detector outcomes are assessed. Engineering decision

Check geometry and detector-support eligibility before interpreting any recovery outcome; preserve the paired control so the background remains comparable.

Still unknown

Expected placement does not guarantee support, recovery, calibrated aircraft scattering, or realistic field multipath.

2

Construct an echo at a declared delay and Doppler

Question

Can target motion be turned into a repeatable radio echo at a known map location?

Result

The implementation derives excess path and rate, applies wideband propagation and fractional delay, rotates phase for Doppler, RMS-normalizes the echo, and adds declared gain.

Why it matters

The known target can be evaluated against a declared delay-Doppler window, not a visual impression of a map.

Engineering decision

Validate target geometry and support eligibility before interpreting downstream candidates or absences.

Evidence

Real-background synthesis helper, current placement maps, and the historical generator validation dashboard. SRC-02A-05/08 · FIG-02A-02/03/04

Method

Use Rtx + Rrx − L for excess range and its rate for Doppler; use phased.WidebandFreeSpace and FIR fractional delay in the real-background flow. SRC-02A-05/10

Remaining uncertainty

Generator placement validation is not a detector result. It does not model every field reflection, calibrated RCS, aircraft aspect change, or measured direct-path-to-target ratio.

7. Test 3: What uncertainty does synthetic truth remove?

Result

The real-background suite copies the real dual-channel recording, adds the echo only to CH1 surveillance for injected cases, preserves CH2 reference, and supplies an unmodified paired control.Why it matters: an injected/control difference can be attributed to the declared change rather than a different background recording.Conclusion: the experiment narrows the question to controlled target placement and downstream response. SRC-02A-05/07

Engineering decision

Use real-background paired cases, preserve manifest/truth provenance, and reserve live-aircraft or collection claims for independent field evidence.

Still unknown

Synthetic truth cannot establish operational Pd/Pfa, map-wide false alarms, a qualified receiver/reference path, tracking, or localization.

3

Hold the recording constant; change only the declared echo

Question

What ambiguity disappears when an injected case is paired with the same real recording before injection?

Result

The injected and control packages share the background. The injected case changes CH1 with the declared target echo; CH2 reference remains preserved.

Why it matters

The experiment can isolate an injection/replay question from scene-to-scene variation and preserve a clear record of what changed.

Engineering decision

Write paired data plus manifests, truth JSON, role metadata, and readback fields so a reviewer can reconstruct the controlled condition.

Evidence

Real-background suite generator, its design note, package manifests, and truth-blind downstream entry points. SRC-02A-05/07/09

Method

Copy recorded channels, inject the calculated echo only in surveillance, retain an untouched control, write the package, then run the detector without truth-driven changes.

Remaining uncertainty

The controlled package cannot replace measured aircraft echo, field antenna/RF validation, operational false-alarm assessment, tracking, or localization evidence.

9. What this method does not prove

Do not promote controlled recovery into a live-aircraft claim. Synthetic truth is powerful because it controls target placement. It does not create a measured aircraft echo or close the separate hardware, field collection, detector, or operational-performance questions.

Not a calibrated aircraft model

The recovered sources do not provide a calibrated aircraft RCS, direct-path-to-target receive-power ratio, or complete field-multipath model.

Not a receiver qualification

Injection happens in recorded data. It does not verify antenna roles, direct-path/reference quality, lock, drops, gain/headroom, or capture integrity.

Not operational performance

A retained candidate near declared truth is not operational Pd/Pfa, a map-wide false-alarm rate, product selection, tracking, or localization.

12. Glossary

Truth

The declared target position, motion, timing, and expected geometry used to create and later evaluate a controlled synthetic case.

Excess path

Transmitter-to-target plus target-to-receiver distance, minus the direct transmitter-to-receiver distance. It determines expected bistatic delay.

Doppler

The frequency change caused by changing path length. In this experiment, excess-path rate determines the declared bistatic Doppler.

Fractional delay

A delay that is not limited to a whole number of samples, allowing an echo to be placed more precisely in time.

Paired control

The same real recording with no declared synthetic echo added. It is a target-specific comparison, not a map-wide false-alarm experiment.

Truth-blind processing

Map and detector steps run without using target truth. Truth is used only after candidates are retained, for evaluation.
