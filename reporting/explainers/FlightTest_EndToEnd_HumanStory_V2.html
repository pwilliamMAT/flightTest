FlightTest: from a broadcast signal to a trustworthy future aircraft measurement

An end-to-end engineering story for a technical manager, a new project engineer, and future report-generation work.

The short version: the program is investigating whether an existing HDTV broadcast can illuminate aircraft while a two-channel receiver listens to the broadcast and the surrounding scene. It has built design rationale, field infrastructure, replayable recordings, a controlled synthetic-truth experiment, and a gated analysis process. Its strongest result is a bounded synthetic recovery study. It has not yet established a field-qualified reference chain, a valid formal map contract, live-aircraft detection, operational probability of detection or false alarm, tracking, or localization.

How to read this document.

Each stage answers one human question, gives the accepted result and decision, names the artifact handed forward, and states the next dependency. “Modeled preference” describes a design conclusion from a model; “implemented decision” describes what was built or put into the evidence process; “proposed future action” is deliberately not a completed result. The nine accepted reports remain the evidence owners.

Passive HDTV in this project, in one decision-oriented paragraph.

An HDTV transmitter is used as an illuminator of opportunity: the team does not transmit radar energy. One receiver path is intended to capture a reference version of the broadcast; the other is intended to capture scene content that may include reflected broadcast energy. Comparing the two produces a delay–Doppler, or cross-ambiguity, map. A bright map feature is not automatically an aircraft measurement: the reference path, map contract, detector, truth separation, and measurement uncertainty must all be validated first. One bistatic link can supply a future measurement opportunity; it is not by itself aircraft localization.

01AChoose a broadcast and geometry worth studying

Human Question

Which illuminator, receiver site, and observation geometry should be investigated, and why is HDTV useful for that investigation?

Result

Historical modeled target-region coverage ranked the Hudson case at 69.91 dB mean FOV SNR versus 57.78 dB for Newton. The retained historical cases used 6 MHz HDTV channels: 548 MHz for Hudson and 596.3 MHz for Newton. The Apple Hill East Garage was a practical prototype-site decision.

Why It Matters

The broadcast, terrain/building line of sight, direct path, surveillance path, and target region jointly determine whether the receiver-design problem is worth solving. A bistatic delay is the excess path of the transmitter–target–receiver route over the direct transmitter–receiver route; Doppler describes relative path-rate change. Those are map coordinates, not yet location coordinates.

Engineering Decision

Modeled preference — historical Hudson’s modeled comparison was preferred for that historical design study. Implemented prototype decision Apple Hill East Garage became the practical prototype setting. Proposed future action Re-establish the exact current transmitter, direct-path, and reference-path conditions before qualifying a field result.

Artifact Produced

Candidate-tower provenance, channel/bandwidth cases, receiver-site and target-region geometry, and modeled signal inputs for the RF budget.

Still Unknown

The current transmitter configuration and field reference-path adequacy. Historical 548/596.3 MHz cases and 01B’s 551/599 MHz model conventions remain documented historical differences; this story does not reconcile them.

Report 01A — Illuminator Selection

Handed forward: candidate frequency, bandwidth, geometry, and modeled signal conditions become receive-chain design inputs—not an installed-transmitter claim.

Key Visual

01BTurn predicted conditions into a receive-chain design

Human Question

How did predicted direct and target signal conditions drive the receive-side RF design?

Result

The RF Budget Analyzer work treated antenna, cable, impedance transition, filter, LNA, and N320 headroom as a single cascade. One retained vendor filter datum is −0.7335926 dB S21 at 599 MHz; the historical cascade uses a 20 dB LANA context.

Why It Matters

A passive system must preserve a usable broadcast reference while handling a much weaker scene path. A good-looking component list cannot establish that the installed cascade has the intended gain, noise, headroom, or reference quality.

Engineering Decision

Modeled preference Use the modeled cascade to reason about cable loss, impedance, filtering, low-noise gain, and SDR headroom together. Implemented design decision Carry the designed architecture into physical hardware. Proposed future action Qualify the installed end-to-end response, lock, transport integrity, and reference adequacy.

Artifact Produced

RF budget, designed architecture, component rationale, and a concrete list of installation/qualification questions.

Still Unknown

Whether installed hardware matches the model at the actual field configuration. Modeled RF performance is not collection qualification.

02Build the collection system and preserve the recording

Report 02 — Hardware and Collection

Handed forward: a replayable real dual-channel recording becomes the background for a controlled synthetic-echo experiment.

Key Visual

02ACreate known aircraft-like truth inside real recordings

Human Question

How can the team know where an aircraft-like echo should be without pretending that a synthetic echo is a live aircraft?

Result

The accepted synthetic framework declares target geometry, amplitude, excess path, delay, Doppler, injection, and paired untouched control before analysis. The 15 km anchor maps to −76.456 µs and +540.5 Hz.

Why It Matters

Known truth lets the program test whether the processing chain can retain a controlled echo in real field background. Truth is withheld from map formation, CFAR, and nonmaximum suppression; it is used only after candidate generation to evaluate an association.

Engineering Decision

Implemented decision Inject declared synthetic echo content into the surveillance side while retaining an untouched paired control and a manifest. Proposed future action Keep seed-backed validation distinct from real-background injection and do not promote either mode to live-aircraft performance.

Artifact Produced

Declared target truth, target geometry and placement, injection manifest, paired controls, and a truth-blind processing contract.

Still Unknown

Calibrated live-aircraft return behavior, operational false-alarm behavior, and any aircraft-performance statistic. Synthetic truth is a controlled method, not an operational trial.

Report 02A — Synthetic Echo Generation

03Use gates to stop a map from becoming an unsupported aircraft claim

Human Question

How does the analysis pipeline turn a paired recording into a future measurement opportunity, and what does each gate protect?

Result

The accepted G1–G10 architecture separates ingest, RF/collection health, synchronization, passive-map formation, mitigation, product freeze, truth context, detection, tracker readiness, and track-to-truth evaluation. Formal downstream gates remain disabled.

Why It Matters

A passive map is an upstream delay–Doppler product, not itself a validated aircraft measurement. CFAR is an adaptive threshold that identifies candidate cells under a stated configuration; NMS keeps representative candidates. A configured CFAR Pfa is not an operational false-alarm result, and truth must not steer these steps.

Engineering Decision

Implemented decision Use explicit gate contracts and truth separation to prevent diagnostics from being promoted into formal detections. Current gate state Formal G5/G6/G8 and later tracking work cannot advance while their upstream requirements are open.

Artifact Produced

G1–G10 contracts, truth-separation rules, and requirements for any downstream release.

Still Unknown

Whether the map meets its formal validity contract and whether a frozen detector input can be selected. Without that, no validated measurement stream exists for a tracker.

04Diagnose the map-rate and direct-path problem without erasing failure evidence

Human Question

Why did map-rate recovery and direct-path mitigation work become necessary, and what did it actually establish?

Result

The formal R5A map-rate matrix remains an authoritative failure: 59 of 108 rows violated one or more frozen criteria. The recovery record distinguishes an earlier bounded R4 success from the later R5A failure.

Why It Matters

Direct broadcast energy can dominate the map. The program must show that native-rate and reduced-rate map products are comparable under a common, frozen contract before a detector operates on them. A narrower success cannot replace a broader failed matrix.

Engineering Decision

Supported diagnostic A native-first, calibration-trained then frozen 16-tap NLMS ordering is a bounded upstream correction candidate. Proposed future action Review adoption as an R4 correction and verify it against the unchanged R4 contract before a separately authorized replacement R5A matrix. Boundary R4 bounded success does not erase R5A failure.

Artifact Produced

R1–R5A recovery record, map-rate diagnosis, mitigation characterization, retained failure evidence, and a bounded correction decision boundary.

Still Unknown

Whether the candidate passes controlled verification and a separately authorized replacement matrix. It is not a selected mitigation product or a formal gate closure.

05Keep the strongest evidence strong by keeping its limits visible

Human Question

What is the strongest current experimental evidence, and what exactly is its scope?

Result

In the fixed controlled synthetic study, native-first frozen NLMS produced post-hoc-associated candidates for 8 of 8 available injected geometries, while the no-mitigation comparison produced 0 of 8. The historical 15 km condition was a support exclusion, not a detector miss.

Why It Matters

The study is a disciplined causal experiment: real field background, surveillance-only injection, fixed CFAR/NMS, paired untouched controls, and truth applied only after candidate retention. It identifies a support-policy consequence and controlled recovery behavior without rewriting the formal gate state.

Engineering Decision

Supported diagnostic Preserve the native-first frozen-NLMS versus no-mitigation contrast as controlled synthetic-recovery evidence. Proposed future action Use it to inform bounded R4 correction review and future formal evidence, not product selection or operational claims.

Artifact Produced

Close-target atlas, declared condition grid, paired-control outcomes, support-exclusion distinction, and exact non-conclusions.

Still Unknown

Live-aircraft detection; operational Pd; operational Pfa; a production support policy; a formal detector result; validated measurements; tracking; and localization.

What the Project Has Demonstrated

Evidence that exists today

  • A measurement-first engineering program with explicit claim boundaries.
  • Historical illuminator/geometry screening and a modeled RF-design rationale.
  • Physical collection infrastructure, named dual-channel package evidence, replay, manifests, and supporting ADS-B/log context.
  • A declared synthetic-target method with truth separation and paired controls in real recorded background.
  • A G1–G10 evidence architecture that deliberately refuses unsupported downstream advancement.
  • A retained R5A failure record and a bounded recovery-candidate characterization.
  • A controlled synthetic recovery contrast: 8/8 native-first frozen-NLMS associations versus 0/8 with no mitigation, under the fixed diagnostic setup.

What those facts do not add up to

  • Live-aircraft detection: the accepted strongest target echo is synthetic, even though the background is real.
  • Operational Pd: eight available deterministic injected geometries are not an operational population or coverage sample.
  • Operational Pfa: configured CFAR Pfa=1e-3 and target-specific matched controls are not map-wide operational false-alarm evidence.
  • Validated measurement generation: the formal map, product-freeze, truth-alignment, and detector gates have not enabled a released measurement contract.
  • Tracking: no validated measurement stream with required uncertainty and continuity exists for a tracker.
  • Localization: no multi-observation localization experiment or accuracy result exists; one bistatic link is not localization.

Evidence to trust, and evidence to label carefully

Trust the accepted reports for their stated scopes. Treat deployed hardware and replay packages as infrastructure evidence; historical models as design evidence; the atlas as controlled diagnostic evidence; and the R5A failure as a retained formal limit. Do not collapse seed-backed synthesis into real-background injection, or historical configuration values into current installation values.

Practical working rule: whenever a result sounds like “detection,” ask whether the target was live or injected, whether the cell was eligible for evaluation, whether truth was withheld until after candidate generation, and whether the formal gate required for a measurement has passed. If any answer is missing, keep the statement at its narrower accepted scope.

What Management Should Understand

Accomplishments

  • The project converted an idea into a traceable series of design, hardware, collection, replay, and controlled-experiment artifacts.
  • It has a strong diagnostic causal result rather than an unsupported performance claim.
  • The gate structure has successfully stopped downstream claims from outrunning the evidence.

Current Risks and Blockers

  • Formal R5A map-rate validity remains failed: 59/108 rows violated frozen criteria.
  • Collection/reference qualification remains a separate open branch.
  • Historical configuration differences must stay visible; they are not silently reconciled by this story.

Recommended Next Decision and Evidence

  • Review whether the native-first frozen-NLMS ordering may be adopted as a bounded R4 correction candidate.
  • If approved, verify it against the unchanged R4 contract before considering a separately authorized replacement R5A matrix.
  • In parallel, obtain reviewed channel-role, reference-path, lock/drop, timing/geometry, and manifest evidence for collection suitability.

Recommended Success Criteria

For the next decision, success is not a new presentation or a tracker demonstration. It is controlled R4 correction verification with the existing contract preserved, plus reviewed collection/reference evidence that supports an explicit suitability decision. Only then can later formal detector and measurement work be considered.

Management interpretation

The program is neither “ready for operations” nor “empty.” It has meaningful upstream infrastructure and diagnostic evidence, with two independent evidence branches that must close before the project can responsibly claim a validated measurement—let alone tracking or localization.
