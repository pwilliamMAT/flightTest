Future deck-build handoff rules

Use existing report visuals only. Prefer the exact figure or table named on each card. Do not regenerate plots, substitute new engineering diagrams, or alter a quantitative value without returning to the accepted owner report.
  • Use the exact report-owned evidence IDs in the storyboard CSV and cross-report evidence catalog.
  • Keep every listed claim boundary adjacent to its management message; do not let a visual imply a broader claim.
  • Do not use the close-target atlas to claim live-aircraft detection, operational Pd/Pfa, tracking, localization, or product selection.
  • Do not use deployment photos or packages to claim qualified collection, RF lock, reference adequacy, or aircraft measurement generation.
  • Resolve any future source conflict through the owning accepted report; record it in storyboard_audit.md rather than silently reconciling values.
